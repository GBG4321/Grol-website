FAQ

Email flows for SaaS, answered

Which email flows matter most for SaaS?
The flows that move revenue are onboarding and activation, trial-to-paid conversion, retention and re-engagement, and expansion or upsell. Most SaaS companies have a welcome email and nothing else - the compounding value lives in the activation and expansion flows that run automatically for every user, forever.
What tools do you build email flows in?
We build in Klaviyo by default, and we can adapt to whatever tool you already use. Either way the flows are built natively in your platform, so you keep full ownership and control.
How long does it take to build lifecycle flows?
Up to 10 days. That includes design - every flow comes fully built and styled, ready to run, not just a plan on paper.
How do email flows actually increase MRR?
Two ways: more trials become paying users, and more paying users stay and spend more. Because the flows are automated, that lift compounds on every new signup without more ad spend - which is why they pair so well with Meta Ads.
Do you write the email copy too?
Yes - strategy, sequencing, copy and the build. We write every email in your voice, tied to a specific user action and a number that matters. Where the data shows a deeper product or positioning issue, that feeds into our product-level advice.
