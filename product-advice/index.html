FAQ

Product-level advice, answered

What is product-level growth advice?
It's the part of growth that can't fit in a dashboard. We watch how real users move through your product and funnel, then tell you what to change about the product, onboarding or positioning so marketing converts better. It's the difference between buying more traffic and fixing why traffic doesn't stick.
How is this different from a marketing agency?
A typical agency optimises the ad and stops at the click. We follow the signal past the click - into activation, onboarding and pricing - because that's usually where SaaS growth is actually won or lost. The ad can only sell what the product and positioning deliver.
What data do you look at?
Session recordings, funnel drop-off, activation and retention cohorts, and the qualitative story behind your ad performance. We watch the sessions - where people hesitate, what they ignore, where they leave - and turn that into specific, prioritised changes.
Will you work directly with our product team?
Yes. We share findings as clear, prioritised recommendations your product and design team can act on, and we'll get on calls with them. We're not trying to own your roadmap - we're handing you the evidence to make better calls on it.
Is product-level advice a separate service or included?
It's woven into how we work. When we run your ads or email flows, we're already reading the product signal - this is the layer where we tell you what it means. For teams who only want the strategic read, we can scope it on its own.
