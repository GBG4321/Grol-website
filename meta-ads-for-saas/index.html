They often tell us

“Meta doesn't work for B2B SaaS”

It does - when it's built right. When it falls flat, it's almost never the channel - it's one of these six mistakes. They're also why most SaaS founders can't crack ads alone, and why the wrong agency quietly burns through your budget:

They don't really know their ICP

Targeting and messaging get aimed at everyone, so they connect with no one.

Tracking is an afterthought

Without solid server-side tracking, the algorithm optimizes half-blind and burns budget.

They run static ads only

No video, no fresh hooks - nothing that stops the scroll or builds genuine interest.

Traffic goes to the homepage

Visitors land on a generic page instead of one built for the exact person who clicked.

They chase only ready-to-buy users

Skipping educational, trust-building content means the other 95% never warm up.

They optimize for the wrong event

Chasing cheap clicks instead of real pipeline teaches the algorithm to find the wrong people.

Fix all six and Meta becomes one of the most scalable acquisition channels a SaaS can have. That's exactly what we build.

FAQ

Meta Ads for SaaS, answered

How much should a SaaS company budget for Meta ads?
It depends on your price point. If you sell a low-ticket product - a cheapest plan up to around $100/month - we can start with about $1,000/month in ad spend and still gather real signal. If your entry price is higher than that, plan on at least $2,000/month so the algorithm has enough conversions to learn from. Management is separate, and we size spend to how fast you need results rather than a fixed package.
How long before Meta ads show results for SaaS?
You'll usually see early signal - cost per trial or demo - within 2-4 weeks once the pixel has enough conversion data. Revenue impact takes one full sales cycle to read honestly, which for most SaaS is 30-90 days. We never judge a campaign on day-three numbers.
Do Meta ads actually work for B2B SaaS?
Yes - the people who buy B2B software are on Instagram and Facebook like everyone else. The difference is the funnel: B2B needs a longer nurture path (lead magnet or low-friction trial, then email and retargeting) rather than expecting a cold click to convert. We build that whole path, often paired with lifecycle email flows, not just the ad.
How do you measure SaaS ad performance?
Against revenue, not vanity metrics. We track cost per qualified trial or demo, trial-to-paid rate, CAC and payback period - tied back through your analytics or CRM. If a campaign can't be connected to pipeline or revenue, we don't keep running it.
