FAQ

Product advice + GTM, answered

What does GTM strategy mean here?
It's the work that sits before and around the ads: understanding your product deeply enough to get the positioning, offer and messaging right, then picking the channels that actually fit your product instead of defaulting to whatever we sell.
Will you tell us Meta isn't the right channel?
Yes, if that's what the evidence says. We'd rather tell you a channel isn't a fit before you spend on it than convince you it is just to start working together. The goal is picking what has the biggest potential for your specific product, not selling our own service first - see how we run Meta Ads when it is the right fit.
What do you mean by improving conversion in the product itself?
We watch how real users move through your site and your product, spot where they hesitate or drop off, and hand you specific, prioritised changes - to your landing pages, onboarding, or pricing - so more of the traffic you already have converts and sticks.
Do we need this before running ads?
Not strictly, but it makes every dollar of ad spend work harder. Positioning and channel-fit decisions made without digging into the product tend to get expensive to unwind later. Getting them right first is usually the cheaper path.
Is this a one-time engagement or ongoing?
Both work. Some teams want a focused GTM sprint before launch or a relaunch; others want us reading the product and market on an ongoing basis alongside the ads and email work. We scope it to what you actually need.
